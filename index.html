Guide to Comparing Domestic Franchises

Compare three well-known South Korean fried-chicken brands using one consistent measure: the number of franchise locations.

Created by: Sarraf Priti

1. What are we comparing?

Industry: Fried-chicken restaurants in South Korea.

Metric: Number of franchise locations (stores), not company revenue or average sales per store.

Data year: 2025 year-end. Publication date and data year are different: a report can be published later but describe earlier figures.

Important: A larger store count does not prove higher profit, lower startup costs, or better quality. This page compares store count only.
