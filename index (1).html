Guide to Comparing Domestic Franchises

Three well-known Korean fried-chicken brands, compared using one simple and consistent measure: the number of franchise locations.

국내 프랜차이즈 비교 가이드: 한국의 대표적인 치킨 브랜드 세 곳을 동일한 기준인 가맹점 수로 비교합니다.

1. What are we comparing?

Industry: Fried-chicken restaurants in South Korea.

Metric: Number of franchise locations (stores), not total company revenue and not average sales per store.

Data year: 2024 year-end. The figures below are reported in a summary of the Korea Fair Trade Commission's franchise statistics. Publication date and data year are different: the report was published in 2026, while the counts describe the end of 2024.

Important: Store count does not tell us whether a franchise is more profitable, cheaper to open, or better for every owner. This page compares only the number of franchise locations.

비교 기준: 한국 치킨 프랜차이즈의 2024년 말 가맹점 수를 비교합니다. 보고서 발행 연도와 데이터 기준 연도는 다를 수 있습니다. 가맹점 수가 많다고 해서 수익성이 더 높다는 뜻은 아닙니다.

2. Meet the three brands

BBQ (비비큐)

2,316 stores

A major Korean fried-chicken franchise with a large domestic store network.

국내에 많은 가맹점을 운영하는 주요 치킨 프랜차이즈입니다.

bhc (비에이치씨)

2,228 stores

A large Korean chicken brand known for fried chicken and seasoned menu options.

후라이드 치킨과 다양한 양념 메뉴로 알려진 대형 치킨 브랜드입니다.

Kyochon Chicken (교촌치킨)

1,361 stores

A Korean chicken franchise especially associated with soy-garlic and other seasoned chicken.

간장·마늘 맛 등 다양한 치킨 메뉴로 알려진 한국 치킨 프랜차이즈입니다.

5. Sources and verification notes

  1. Korea Fair Trade Commission (KFTC) — Franchise disclosure comparison portal
    Official tool for checking franchise disclosure information, including store counts and average franchisee sales by year. Use it to verify each brand's disclosure record.
  2. KFTC — 2024 franchise business statistics announcement (published April 2025)
    Official background source explaining how franchise statistics are compiled and why the report's publication year may differ from the year of the store-count data.
  3. Franchise statistics summary PDF attachment
    The search-indexed summary lists the 2024 year-end chicken-brand franchise counts used in this comparison. The link is hosted by a university notice page rather than the KFTC domain, so use the official KFTC portal above for cross-checking.
  4. KFTC — Public information and franchise-related statistics
    Official directory for franchise disclosure records and franchise statistics.
Verification note: Before submitting, open the KFTC comparison portal and search each brand. If the official portal shows a different count or base year, update the table and cite the exact official record. Do not substitute corporate revenue for franchise-location counts.
